{% call u.caveat("Why a state's share of stories is not a measure of news") %}

A story is a relevant, unique item the classifier tagged with that state. One item can count in several states, and {{ totals.no_state|u_int }} unique relevant items in this window have no state tag. The share compares a state's stories with its share of House seats in ref.races ({{ total_seats }} rows for the places listed, including DC's non-voting delegate). Seats stand in for population, because the gazetteer has no population column. A high ratio can mean a contested statewide race as easily as more sources.
