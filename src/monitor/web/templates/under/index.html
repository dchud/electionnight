{% call u.caveat("What to keep in mind on every page") %}
  • Rows are not records. Tables are append-only, so a correction or a recomputed bin is a new row. Readers take the newest row per key.
  • {{ overview.counts.backlog|u_int }} of {{ overview.counts["items"]|u_int }} items were published over 2 hours before they were first seen, most of them feed backlog from the first fetch at {{ overview.counts.first_seen|u_et }}. Feeds serve their newest entries, not a complete history.
  • Dashboard panels read an in-memory fact table in the web process, not the gold.* tables. The dashboard's time axis places an item at its published time in two cases: it was published over 2 hours before first seen, or it was published around a collection gap and first seen just after the gap. gold.* bins use first_seen_at unchanged, so the two can differ.
  • Capture of traces, raw entries, and decisions started on the afternoon of Nov 1. Older items lack them, which is expected.
