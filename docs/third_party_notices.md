## Data

### AllSides Media Bias Ratings

`config/lean_ratings.csv` and the `/sources` page hold labels from AllSides.

> AllSides Media Bias Ratings™ by AllSides.com are licensed under a Creative
> Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License. These ratings
> may be used for research or noncommercial purposes with attribution.

Citation: AllSides Media Bias Ratings™. AllSides Technologies, Inc.
<https://www.allsides.com/media-bias/media-bias-ratings>. Retrieved November
2026.

License: [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). This
project abbreviates the five labels as L, LL, C, LR, and R. The labels were
read from search results.

### Race ratings

`config/races.yaml` holds race ratings copied from Wikipedia's tables. The
ratings belong to their publishers: Cook Political Report, Inside Elections,
Sabato's Crystal Ball, Decision Desk HQ, Fox News, RealClearPolitics, Silver
Bulletin, The Economist, FiftyPlusOne, Split Ticket, NBC News, and MS NOW. This
project holds no license from these publishers.

### Wikipedia

The race, candidate, and entity data comes from English Wikipedia, whose text
is available under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
`tests/fixtures/ref/README.md` lists the articles excerpted in the test
fixtures.

### US government data

Census Bureau and Federal Election Commission data are US government works.
The Census Bureau is the source of the place and map data.

### News

Headlines, summaries, and article text belong to their publishers. The screens
in the [tour](tour.md) show a few headlines. Article text and original pages
stay in the local lake and archive files, and in a private bucket when one is
configured. They are not in this repository.
